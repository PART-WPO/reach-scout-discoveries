# REACH Scout Preprint Watchlist — first of each month

Track preprints across **all Scout discovery routes**: monthly DataCite/Crossref, historical DataCite, historical Crossref, independent discovery, API-seeded expansion and manuscript-version follow-ups. Crossref posted-content and DataCite explicit preprint metadata are publication-status holds, not REACH outputs. Retain previously verified preprint leads. Recheck the tracked preprint on the **1st of each month**, including weekends, using the existing free GitHub Actions infrastructure. The regular hourly Scout missions remain weekday-only.

Every new Scout reviewed-feed candidate (including independent and historical candidates) is checked via both registries in the universal preprint intake gate before publication. Unknown or inaccessible official-publication evidence keeps an article on hold instead of assuming it is published; non-publication dataset, instrument and software discovery remains independent. Newly identified independent metadata leads are registered immediately without resetting the monthly recheck clock. A preprint DOI is ineligible for the new-output Discovery Hub workflow. Treat online-first journal versions of record as published even if print comes later; continue to use the established Crossref print-preferred publication-date policy. Crossref created dates and preprint posted dates never prove journal publication.

Only an explicit isPreprintOf (or DataCite IsPreprintOf) DOI relationship plus a verified Crossref journal-article with real published/issued metadata creates a final-article DOI *research lead*. A plausible title match alone is not proof. Preserve the preprint original, checked-source evidence and any metadata outages; neither a preprint nor a linked final DOI is automatically staged. Final journal DOI requires complete REACH and Scout duplicate/version screening, verified specific WPO funding/project and required metadata, then the existing human review process.

Independent DOI-seeded related-output research remains eligible even when the preprint is on hold. Do not rewrite immutable source batches, existing reviewer decisions, or source DOI identities. Legacy public feed mistakes are corrected with SHA-verified supersession that removes only held preprint DOI from active future proposal feeds; it does not cancel private reviewer decisions already made. Public GitHub metadata must never contain private reviewer details.

## November 2 lightweight intake classification and mission-budget rule (supersedes redundant universal registry lookups)

Classify preprint status at the **first substantive Crossref/DataCite DOI metadata retrieval**, using its authoritative work type and explicit preprint relationship. Crossref `posted-content` and DataCite explicit `Preprint` are held and added/deduplicated in the persistent watchlist, never proposed as NEW OUTPUT. A valid final journal article is not a preprint merely because a related preprint exists. When the authoritative initial DOI response already establishes status, **reuse the evidence**; do not issue duplicate Crossref+DataCite calls merely to repeat the classification. For genuinely ambiguous or contradictory article status, use one targeted authoritative verification and hold unresolved cases; fail closed before feed publication.

The **only scheduled watchlist publication recheck** is the existing first-of-month GitHub Action, including weekend month starts. Ordinary hourly missions do not enumerate/recheck all watchlist entries or conduct a separate preprint sweep. They may register newly encountered preprints and consume already-verified final-article leads from the monthly checkpoint in the normal priority queue. Preserve original DOI, isPreprintOf evidence, final DOI, publication metadata and human decisions; no auto-promotion.

Hourly briefing: one short exception-oriented line with newly held preprints and newly verified published leads **only if changed**; omit repeated full inventory counts and lengthy watchlist commentary. Detailed counts remain available in the monthly checkpoint and operational ledger. Preprint safeguards must not preempt historical Crossref original/expansion research.
